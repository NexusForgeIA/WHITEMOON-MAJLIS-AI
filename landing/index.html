    Questions

    Straight answers

    Does the customer know it is an AI?

    Yes. The chat is labelled “AI assistant” and says so in its first message.

    What if it does not know the answer?

    It says so and offers to pass the customer to a person. It never invents prices or medical, legal or financial advice.

    Does it guarantee more sales?

    No. It answers faster and never misses a message. What you earn from that depends on your business.

    Where does it run?

    On your website, or through a QR code or link. Other channels can come later.
