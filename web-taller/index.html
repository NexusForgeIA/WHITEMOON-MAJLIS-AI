AI assistant

Leo books your visit without a phone call

Leo is the workshop's AI assistant. Tell it what your car needs, in English or Arabic, and it asks only for your name and a Qatar mobile number. Then it shows you the slots that are actually free.

When you pick a time, the booking is written into the workshop calendar and you get a summary with the date, time and service. If you would rather speak to a person, Leo passes your details to the team.
